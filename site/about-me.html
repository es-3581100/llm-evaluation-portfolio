About / path

About me

My path into AI and software began in operations, not computer science.

I started in people- and process-heavy work, including retail operations and management at Harps, where the job was keeping people aligned and systems moving. At Volta, I worked across fractional distillation lab operations and the business side of taking a product from import through processing to the shelf. Later, warehouse and distribution work made dependencies impossible to ignore: inventory, timing, routing, software, equipment, labor, and information all have to agree for the operation to work. I became increasingly interested in how those systems were structured and how better information and automation could improve them.

AI turned a casual interest into serious self-directed study and building. I moved from individual models and small tools toward software architecture, agent orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, provenance, developer tooling, and the safe coordination of multiple models with conventional software. That progression is why I’m now shifting my career toward data, AI, LLM systems, and software and systems engineering.

Strength

Systems thinking. I tend to map what depends on what, where information originates, which component has authority, where failures can propagate, and what evidence shows that something actually worked. My operations background also keeps me focused on the gap between a technically correct system and one people can actually use.

Gaps

Taking a nontraditional route left some areas to strengthen, including deeper CS fundamentals, statistics, production data engineering, distributed systems, and cloud infrastructure. I treat those as areas of ongoing study rather than things to work around.

Principle

AI should not replace engineering discipline. I use models heavily for exploration, implementation, debugging, and review, but I want the runtime underneath that work to rest on explicit interfaces, tests, deterministic validation, logging, version control, bounded permissions, and human authority where it matters. The goal is AI operating inside systems whose important behavior can still be inspected, tested, and understood.

Goal

Combine my operational perspective with increasingly rigorous technical skills and grow into applied AI, data systems, LLM infrastructure, agent engineering, developer tooling, and related systems work.
