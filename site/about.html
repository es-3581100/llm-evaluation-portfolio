About / trajectory

From evaluation into systems.

I’m an experimental AI systems builder with a nontraditional path into software and a habit of turning abstract problems into inspectable working structures.

My earlier portfolio centered on LLM evaluation, retrieval quality, adversarial testing, persistent-agent reliability, and safe tool use. That work still matters; it taught me to separate evidence from confidence and to keep claims bounded by what a system actually demonstrated.

The work has since widened. I now spend much of my time designing agent control planes, AI-native applications, model-aware developer systems, provenance infrastructure, technical visualizations, and cross-language experiments. I’m especially interested in the translation layers between model and verifier, agent and runtime, mathematics and executable form, repository and semantic map, and human intent and useful software.

I use AI heavily in the build process, but model output is not treated as independent evidence. The engineering goal is to make increasingly capable workflows more inspectable, reproducible, and useful—not merely more autonomous.
